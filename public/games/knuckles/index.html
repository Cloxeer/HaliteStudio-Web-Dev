How to play

Four moves. Zero mercy.

Two 3 by 3 boards face off. Take turns rolling one die and dropping it into a column. When a board is full, the game is over.

  1. Roll

    Roll one die, 1 through 6. Every turn.

  2. Drop

    Pick a column on your board. Three dice fill it.

  3. Smash

    Every matching face in their same column gets knocked out.

  4. Score

    Matching dice in a column multiply. High score takes the table.

5 pts
One 5 ×1
20 pts
Two 5s ×4
45 pts
Three 5s ×9

The game ends when a board is full, and the highest score wins.
